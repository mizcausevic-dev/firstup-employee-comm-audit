# Kinetic Gain Embedded Tie-Back

`firstup-employee-comm-audit` is the public proof surface for a deeper embedded product pattern: audience evidence, approval routing, localization readiness, and send-safe delivery posture inside enterprise employee communications workflows.

## Embedded angle

- ingest campaign and audience snapshots from employee comms systems and adjacent HR/security tooling
- surface missing approvals, stale localization packets, and identity-sync blockers before sensitive sends go live
- attach evidence packets and escalation history to each communication lane
- expose review-safe dashboards for internal comms, people operations, security, and compliance stakeholders

- CISO / HR technology lead
- Head of Internal Communications
- People Operations platform owner
- Employee experience / digital workplace leader
